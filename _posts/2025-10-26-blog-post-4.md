Let us consider kinematics of the racing car
<div align="center">
$$\begin{align}
    x_{t+1} = f_p(x_t, u_t;\zeta_p) =
    \begin{bmatrix}
    X_t + \Delta_T v_t \cos \phi_t,
    \\
    Y_t + \Delta_T v_t \sin \phi_t,
    \\
    \phi_t + \Delta_T  \omega_t,
    \\
    v_t + \Delta_T \zeta_p a_t,
    \end{bmatrix}
\end{align}$$
</div>
where $$\zeta_p \in (0,1]$$, $$\Delta_T = 0.05[s]$$ is the slipping coefficient which depends position of the racing car in the track (or road).
Denote by
$$x_t = [X_t, Y_t, \phi_t, v_t]^\top$$, $$u_t = [\omega_t, a_t]^\top$$ the state of the racing car at current time step $$t$$.



The reference path of an MPCC controller is parameterized by a progress variable,
representing a parametric curve (lap) in the global $$\mathrm{X}$$-$$\mathrm{Y}$$ frame, with coordinates given by the functions $$X_\text{cen}(p)$$ and $$Y_\text{cen}(p)$$ defined over $$p \in [0, p_{\max}]$$ so that $$X_\text{cen}(0) = X_\text{cen}(p_{\max})$$ and $$Y_\text{cen}(0) = Y_\text{cen}(p_{\max})$$. 
The curve is the interpolation of many way points. The $$p_{\max}$$ denotes the maximum progress for a lap, and $$p_t$$ denotes the current progress, and $$\bar{p}_t =  p_t \% p_{\max}$$ denote location of car in the current lap.


In this paper, we assume that $$X_\text{cen}(p)$$ and $$Y_\text{cen}(p)$$ describe the racetrack centerline, obtained via interpolation between successive track waypoints.
The vehicle completes the track as $$p$$ increases.
At the current position $$[X_t,~Y_t]$$, $$p_t$$ is determined as
<div align="center">
$$
\begin{equation*}
\text{OP0}:\;
    \bar{p}_t = \text{argmin}_{p \in [0,\; p_{\max}]}~ \Vert X_t - X_\text{cen}(p)\Vert_2^2 + \Vert Y_t - Y_\text{cen}(p)\Vert_2^2  \text. 
\end{equation*}
$$
</div>
The lag error $$e_\text{lag}$$ and contour error $$e_\text{cot}$$ {are defined based on the} position $$(X, Y)$$ and progress $$p$$ {as}
<div align="center">
$$
\begin{align}
    e_\text{cot} (x_t, \bar{p}_t) &=  \sin(\Phi(\bar{p}_t))(X_t - X_\text{cen}(\bar{p}_t)) - \cos(\Phi(\bar{p}_t))(Y_t - Y_\text{cen}(\bar{p}_t)), \notag 
    \\
    e_\text{lag}(x_t, \bar{p}_t) &=  - \cos(\Phi(\bar{p}_t))(X_t - X_\text{cen}(\bar{p}_t))  - \sin(\Phi(\bar{p}_t))(Y_t - Y_\text{cen}(\bar{p}_t)), \notag 
\end{align}
$$
</div>
where $$\Phi(\bar{p}_t) = \arctan\big(\nabla Y_\text{cen}(\bar{p}_t)/\nabla  X_\text{cen}(\bar{p}_t)\big)$$ is the tangent angle of the reference path, and
<div align="center">
$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{OP1:}\;J_H^{\star}(x_t, \theta, \zeta_p) = \min  &\; \sum_{i = 1}^{H} c_i,\\
    \text{s.t.}\;
    &x_{i+1|t} = f_p(x_{i|t}, u_{i|t};\zeta_p), \; x_{0|t} = x_t \text, 
    \\
    & p_{i+1|t} = p_{i|t} + \vartheta_{i|t} \text,\; p_{0|t} = p_t, \; \bar{p}_{i|t} = p_{i|t} \% p_{\max}
    \\
    &p_{i|t} \geq 0, \; T_s v_{i|t} \geq \vartheta_{i|t} \geq 0,
    \\
    &50 \geq a_{i|t} \geq -50,~ 3.2 \geq \omega_{i|t} \geq -3.2,~ 
    \\
    &(X_{i|t} - X_\text{cen}(p_{i|t}))^2 +  (Y_{i|t} - Y_\text{cen}(p_{i|t}))^2 \leq \Delta_{\max}^2,
\end{aligned}
$$
</div>
where 
$$c_i =q_\text{c} e_\text{cot}^2(x_{i|t}, \bar{p}_{i|t})  + q_\ell e_\text{lag}^2(x_{i|t},  \bar{p}_{i|t}) + q_\omega \omega_{i-1|t}^2 + q_a a_{i-1|t} ^2 - q_\text{v} \vartheta_{i-1|t}$$,
$$\theta = q_v$$, $$q_\omega = 0.01, q_a = 0.01$$,  $$q_l = 1000$$, $$q_c = 1$$. 
In this problem let use select $$H = 20$$,
use Casadi to solve the MPCC problem, and select $$p_{\max}$$ is actual arc length of the map, $$\Delta_\text{max} = 1.1$$,
